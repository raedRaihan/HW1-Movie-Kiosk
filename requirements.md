1. Customers can view which movies are available and their associated showtimes using the system.
2. Customers can select their seats after they choose a movie from the available seats for that movie.
3. The system makes sure that customers are not able to reserve a seat that has already been reserved.
4. Customers can purchase a ticket after they have selected their movie and seat.
5. The system sends a confirmation email after a customer purchases a ticket.
